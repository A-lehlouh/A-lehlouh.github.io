Kuwaiti Arabic LLM Fine-Tuning

Adapted LLaMA-2 7B to understand and generate Kuwaiti dialect Arabic, a variety general models handle poorly. I built the full pipeline: JSONL dataset loading, tokenization, a 90/10 train/test split, 3-epoch FP16 training, and inference for text generation.

  • 449Kuwaiti dialect training samples
  • r=8, α=32LoRA adapters instead of full fine-tuning
  • 8-bitquantization to fit a 7B model in limited GPU memory
